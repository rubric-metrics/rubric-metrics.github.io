Rubric Metrics

Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Who we are

Rubric Metrics is a small project run by one person in Texas, USA. It publishes short videos on Instagram (@rubric.metrics). Questions about this policy go to rubric.metrics@gmail.com.

What this website collects

Nothing. This site has no accounts, forms, cookies, analytics, or advertising tools, and we do not ask you for any personal information to read it.

Hosting

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when it serves a page, under its own privacy statement. We do not use that data.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell or share them. You can ask us to delete your message at any time.

Instagram

Our videos are on Instagram. Your likes, comments, follows, and other activity there are handled by Meta under its own policies. For our own account, we use Instagram's aggregate analytics (such as reach, plays, and saves for each video) to learn which topics work. We do not collect personal data about individual viewers.

Other data we use

We use aggregate Google Trends search-interest figures to choose topics. These figures contain no personal data about the people who searched.

Tools we use to make videos

We use AI and media services to write, voice, and render videos. Only topic and script text is sent to them. No visitor data is sent to them.

Children

This site and our videos are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your choices

Because this site collects no personal data, there is little to access or delete. If you have emailed us and want your message removed, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
