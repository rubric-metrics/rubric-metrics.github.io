Rubric Metrics

Short videos where every factual claim traces to a cited source.

What this is

Rubric Metrics is a small project run by one person. It makes 60 to 75 second spoken-word videos for Instagram (@rubric.metrics) about topics people are curious about right now. The videos are AI-generated, and each one is labeled as AI-generated on Instagram.

How a video is made

Each topic is researched first. Every fact has to come from a primary source or an established news outlet, with a quote to back it. A separate check then reads the script sentence by sentence and ties each factual claim to its source. A risk review screens topics before anything is published, and some topics are off limits, such as elections, scandals about named people, and conspiracy or extremism content.

How Google Trends data is used

Aggregate Google Trends search-interest data helps decide which topic ideas are worth making first. The project uses only the aggregate interest figures. It does not collect or use personal data about people who search.

About this site

This site has no accounts, forms, or tracking. Videos are for general information and are not medical, legal, or financial advice.
